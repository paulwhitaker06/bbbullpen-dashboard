Methodology & sources

Bullpen rankings

Three forward-looking ingredients, each normalized 0–100 across the 30 teams, then averaged: LI-weighted projected FIP (closer 1.7×, primary setup 1.4×, secondary setup 1.2×, middle 1.0×); projected ROS WAR (Steamer/ZiPS blend, captures depth); current-season Pitching+ (Stuff+ × Location+).

Phase 2 — Editorial + availability layer

ESPN closer org chart parses each team's IL list and editorial section. Razzball pitch-count chart tags pitchers as "tired" via the deterministic ESPN formula (≥25 pitches yesterday, ≥35 over 3 days, or back-to-back appearances). Both layers REMOVE pitchers from the top-5 before composite math, then refill from FG slots 6-8. Watchlist captures every removal.

Phase 3 — Pitcher List math layer

RP Roundup parser scans editorial sentences for Tier A signals (velocity/stuff/command decline → 0.7× LI-weight discount, single mention triggers) and Tier B (concerning/shaky/wobbly → requires 2+ mentions in 7 days). Recovery patterns (welcome back, first save since) undo active discounts. Closed-set pitcher-name matching against the FG roster. Persistence log at pl_history.jsonl.

Live alerter — the only bet trigger

The alerter (`bullpen/alerter.py`) fires on live games matching all of: inning ≥ 7, score within 1 run, FIP- gap ≥ 20 between bullpens, BE-favored team is leading or tied (trailing-favorite subset filtered out per Tier 3+ finding — it's a 38% comeback rate, money-loser). Per-game dedupe stops spam; fatigue + live pen-burn already burned arms inform but don't recompute BE (Tier 3++ research showed burn-adjustment slightly hurts prediction).

Candidates Tonight

Pre-game preview of games whose FIP- gap is wide enough that a close late-inning state would clear the alerter's filter. Just the schedule + composite gap; if the matchup goes ≤1 run in the 7th the alerter fires.

Phase 7 status — empirical K calibration

Tier 1 (4,857 games, 2024+2025): naive K = 0.182. Tier 2 (with team-strength controls): K_LPM ≈ 0 — looked like the thesis was dead. Tier 3+ (methodology fix): the "composites" had been built from each team's top-5 by IP, which is the starting rotation, not the bullpen. Once rebuilt on real reliever data only and restricted to the live alerter's trigger: K_LPM = +0.232 (lead-controlled +0.202, p<0.001), bet-population win rate 60.5%. By lead state: leads 81%, tied 59% (cleanest edge), trails 38% (filtered out). Tier 3+++ (FIP- cutover): refit on the absolute FIP- scale gave K = +0.39 with cross-season drift of just 0.025 (vs 0.076 on the composite scale). Production K = 0.39 since v1.7. See research/k_calibration/reports/fip-minus-k.md.
